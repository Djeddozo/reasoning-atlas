# RA-071 — Vision → Goal → Task, deadlines instead of time layers

- UTC timestamp: 2026-10-25T15:55Z · Agent: Claude
- Owner (2026-10-25): the year, quarter, month and week goals made no sense to him. Asked whether they were functional: only partly (a year or quarter objective fed the Focus recommendation; month and week were context; none changed the ranking of tasks). He chose: Vision → Goal → Task, with a deadline he sets himself, so that only the deadline raises priority; goals are edited in Focus.

## Completed
- `goals.due` (migration); new goals have horizon `goal` (a vision stays `life`); old time layers are read as plain goals and no deadlines were invented for them.
- `priority.goal_deadlines` / `effective_due`: a task's urgency uses the earlier of its own deadline and its goal's (inherited from goals above); the explanation says "(its goal's deadline)".
- `compass`: a goal is "behind" only against its deadline; `add_objective(..., due)`, `set_due` (undoable, re-ranks at once). Hub `engine.direction.shape_directions`: goal with deadline, `due`, `rename`, `drop`.
- Brain sorting: a goal gets a deadline only when the owner names a date; a lifetime aim under a vision becomes a goal. Do-over may not invent deadlines.
- Focus: per vision its goals with a date picker, rename, set aside, and "Add goal" with an optional deadline; the timeline appears only with a deadline. Forge › Plan: no "+ quarter goal" buttons; a goal shows "vision" or its deadline.

## Checks
- tests/test_compass.py test_only_a_deadline_raises_priority (a time layer alone changes nothing; a goal deadline raises its task and is explained; a bad date refused; clearing restores); updated recommendation test; tests/test_talk.py (deadline only when named); full suite.
- Browser: a goal with a deadline added in Focus shows "due" and its timeline.
