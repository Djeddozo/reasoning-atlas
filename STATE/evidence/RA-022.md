## Completed
- `services/atlas/capabilities.py`: the gateway.
  - Looks a capability up in the registry; refuses unknown ids (404), capabilities that are planned or built but not tested (409 `unavailable`), and modules Atlas has no adapter for (409 `unreachable`).
  - `read`: runs at once through the module's adapter. For the Engine that is `HubAdapter` (hub on the Pi, `services/atlas/hubclient.py`). Every answer, including failures, carries provenance: callId, capabilityId, module, moduleName, effect, via, version (the hub's running commit, or `hub protocol v1 (commit unknown)` when the hub cannot read its commit), time, source file and summary.
  - `propose`: `preview` stores a pending call and sends nothing. `confirm` claims it and sends it once (write-ahead: the call is marked `running` before it is sent). Previewing the same proposal again reuses the pending preview. Confirming again replays the stored outcome. A proposal identical to one already sent is refused (409 `duplicate`) unless the owner confirms with `repeat`. A call left `running` (Atlas stopped mid-call) is never sent again on its own. Previews expire after 15 minutes (410).
  - `act`: always refused (403 `disabled`), logged. The adapter is never reached.
  - Offline: when the hub is not configured, unreachable or refuses the token, the call fails at once with `not_configured`/`offline`/`unauthorized`, a message and provenance. Nothing is substituted. A preview is refused while the module is offline, so the owner is not asked to confirm something that cannot be sent.
  - Call log: table `capability_calls` in Atlas's own database (`runtime/atlas.db`). Read results are recorded only as a SHA-256, not stored. Proposal results up to 20 kB are stored so they can be replayed.
  - `audit(db)`: checks every logged call for complete provenance and reports the gaps.
- `services/atlas/server.py`: `GET /api/capabilities/call/<id>?<inputs>`, `POST /api/capabilities/preview {id, inputs}`, `POST /api/capabilities/confirm {callId, repeat?}`, `GET /api/capabilities/calls`, `GET /api/capabilities/audit`. Same loopback/Tailscale and Origin checks as the other endpoints.
- `services/atlas/hubclient.py`: health now also returns the hub's protocol version.
- `modules/atlas.json`: new capability `atlas.registry.call` (propose, tested, evidence tests/test_capabilities.py, section Systems).

## Limitations
- The screen does not use the gateway yet. Sections still call `/api/engine/<id>` directly, including the act buttons the owner approved one by one in earlier tasks. Moving each section onto `/api/capabilities/*`, and showing previews in the UI, is the follow-up. Act buttons then need a per-capability allowlist with owner approval (docs/PLATFORM.md).
- The only adapter is the Engine's hub. Atlas's own capabilities (`atlas.notes.list`, …) are answered as `unreachable` through the gateway, because their sections call them directly.
- Read inputs travel as a query string. Lists and objects are refused for reads.
- The call log is not shown on the screen yet. It belongs to the Systems view (RA-021).
- Not deployed. Implemented and tested in this worktree only.
