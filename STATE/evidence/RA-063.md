## Completed
- Still worth building now? Just before a build the overlap check always asks Claude (one call, the one it already made for close matches) and passes the vision in focus with its objectives and the top of the plan. Verdict build / hold / drop with a reason, stored with the check (new columns worth, worth_why). Hold or drop asks once on Today ("Build anyway | Not now | Drop it"); Not now keeps the builder away until the task changes; Build now in the Forge always builds.
- Re-evaluations on Today are short: the approval title says "Plan re-evaluation: 1 changed, 1 dropped (1 kept)"; Claude is asked for at most two plain sentences on what changes mainly and why (drift reasons stay in the log); "Show the changes" unfolds the changed tasks with their reasons (hub read `engine.build.do_over_changes`).
- The manual buttons stay in Forge › Plan for when you want one now.
